Use **Redo with objections…** when a compiled rule is incomplete or misreads its source.

<h2 id="submit-an-objection">Submit an objection</h2>

1. Open a collection's **Rules** tab and expand the rule.
2. Choose **Redo with objections…**.
3. Describe the problem in the **Objection** field, then choose **Submit**.
4. Read the regenerated body and check its page references.

<h2 id="expected-result">Expected result</h2>

Chronacle regenerates that one rule from matching rules material, its current body, and all objections saved for the entry. The new objection is stored for later redos. Existing **Table notes** remain unchanged.

<h2 id="example">Example</h2>

For **Moon-Tide Crossing**, enter:

> The summary omits that the pilot checks the tide before the passengers choose a route. Keep those steps in that order.

After **Submit**, compare the revised procedure with the cited page and keep your house ruling in **Table notes**.

<h2 id="limits">Limits</h2>

- An objection guides generated text; it does not prove the revision is correct.
- If the redo fails, the dialog shows **Failed to redo: …** and the current entry remains available.
- Use **Cancel** to close the objection form without submitting.
