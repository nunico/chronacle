Compiled rules are grouped as **Mechanic**, **Ability**, **State**, **Procedure**, **Resource**, **Statistic**, or **Entry**.

<h2 id="seven-types">Seven types</h2>

- **Mechanic**: a core interaction, such as resolving opposed actions.
- **Ability**: a specific action or spell, such as Veil Step.
- **State**: a condition affecting someone, such as Dazed.
- **Procedure**: ordered table steps, such as resolving a voyage.
- **Resource**: something spent and regained, such as Focus.
- **Statistic**: a value read or changed by rules, such as Guard.
- **Entry**: useful material that does not fit the other six.

<h2 id="browse-and-check">Browse and check</h2>

Open **Campaign & sources**, expand a collection, and choose **Rules**. Use **Search rules**, then expand a name to read its body, page references, and **Table notes**.

<h2 id="example">Example</h2>

Search for **Veil Step**. Chronacle may place it under **Ability** and cite `Field Guide.pdf p.18`. Expand it, compare the summary with page 18, and add “Cannot cross running water in our game” under **Table notes**.

<h2 id="tips">Tips</h2>

- Unknown generated categories fall back to **Entry**.
- **Resource** is spent and recovered; **Statistic** is read or modified.
- Category and summary are generated, so verify the page reference.
