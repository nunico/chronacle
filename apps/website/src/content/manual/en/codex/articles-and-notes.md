Treat a **Codex Article** as generated reference text and **Notes** or **Table notes** as your durable table record.

<h2 id="know-the-boundary">Know the boundary</h2>

Compiling can replace an entity's **Codex Article** and a rule's generated body. Editing an entity's summary or notes marks its article stale, but compiling preserves those user-owned fields. Rule **Table notes** are also preserved when a rule is updated or redone.

<h2 id="record-your-version">Record your version</h2>

1. Open an entity under **Library → Entities**.
2. Put established facts in **Summary** and table-specific detail in **Notes**.
3. Use `[[Entity Name]]` in notes to link another saved entity.
4. For a compiled rule, expand it and type your ruling in **Table notes**. Move focus to another control in that rule to request a save.

<h2 id="example">Example</h2>

The generated **Mara Venn** article says she avoids North Quay. Your group persuaded her otherwise, so add “Now docks at North Quay after sunset” to **Notes**. Recompiling may rewrite the article, but that note remains.

<h2 id="tips">Tips</h2>

- Do not put table canon only in generated prose.
- A direct entity edit makes its generated article stale until you recompile.
- See [Take campaign notes](/en/manual/notes-and-sessions/notes) for entity kinds and linking.

<h2 id="recover-a-table-note">Recover a table note</h2>

Moving focus to another control in the same rule requests an automatic save for **Table notes**. Moving directly to another rule or view retains the note as **Unsaved changes** without treating navigation as a save. Its status also distinguishes **Saving…**, **Saved**, and **Couldn't save**. A failed note remains attached to its rule, even when you collapse it or move elsewhere; choose **Retry** to save that text to the same rule. For the shared recovery and app-close rules, see [Keep drafts and recover saves](/en/manual/notes-and-sessions/saving-and-recovery).
