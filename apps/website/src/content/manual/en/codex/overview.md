The Codex turns source passages into browsable drafts about entities and rules while keeping your own notes separate.

<h2 id="what-you-will-find">What you will find</h2>

- **Codex Article** on an entity: generated from relevant source passages when you compile.
- **Notes** on an entity and **Table notes** on a rule: writing you control.
- **Rules** inside each collection: generated entries grouped by rule kind with page references.

<h2 id="use-the-codex">Use the Codex</h2>

1. Open **Campaign & sources** and choose a subscribed collection.
2. Choose **Compile** when it contains new or stale material.
3. Browse entities under **Library → Entities**, or expand the collection's **Rules** tab.
4. Check the cited page before relying on exact wording at the table.

<h2 id="example">Example</h2>

Compile **Greyharbor Gazetteer**. Open the entity **Mara Venn** to read a generated summary of her ferry route, then add your own note: “Promised the crew passage at moonrise.” The generated article may cite `Greyharbor Gazetteer.pdf p.42`; your note remains your record of play.

<h2 id="keep-in-mind">Keep in mind</h2>

- Generated text is a useful draft, not a guarantee of correctness.
- A **Stale** label means an article needs compiling again after its inputs changed.
- See [Articles and notes](/en/manual/codex/articles-and-notes) for the ownership boundary.
