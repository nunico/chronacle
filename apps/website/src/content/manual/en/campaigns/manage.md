Use **Manage campaigns** to create, select, rename, or delete a campaign without changing shared source collections.

<h2 id="create-or-edit">Create or edit</h2>

1. Open **Campaign & sources**, then expand **Manage campaigns**.
2. Enter a **New campaign name** and optional **System**, then choose **Create**. Chronacle also creates and subscribes a same-named collection for that campaign's own material.
3. Choose a campaign name to make it active. Use **Rename** to edit its **Name** or **System (optional)**, then **Save**.

<h2 id="delete-safely">Delete safely</h2>

Choose the trash button and confirm one of two outcomes: **Delete campaign and its notes** removes the campaign's own collection and its contents, while **Keep notes as a regular collection** keeps that collection as a shared collection. The keep choice removes relationships whose two ends are both inside that collection and records findings for later review. It does not move campaign-scoped notebook entries into the kept collection; those entries are no longer available after the campaign is deleted. Other subscribed shared collections are not deleted. **Cancel** changes nothing.

<h2 id="example">Example</h2>

Create **The Ember Archive** with system **D12 home rules**, then add `Ember Archive Field Notes.pdf` to its own collection. If the game ends but you want to keep that collection-based material, choose **Keep notes as a regular collection**. The collection remains available for later subscriptions, but its internal relationships are removed and need review in **Maintenance**.

<h2 id="remember">Remember</h2>

- Renaming a campaign does not rename its previously created collection.
- The name is required; the system is optional.
- Copy any campaign-scoped notebook text you still need before deleting the campaign.
- Deletion cannot be undone in this screen, so read the two choices carefully.
