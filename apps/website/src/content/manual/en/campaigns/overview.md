A campaign is the working context for one game: it keeps that game's entities, sessions, and chat separate and searches the source collections you subscribe to it.

<h2 id="choose-a-campaign">Choose a campaign</h2>

1. Use the campaign rail on the left to choose your game.
2. Open **Campaign & sources** to review its details and collection subscriptions.
3. Return to **Oracle** before asking a campaign-specific question.

<h2 id="expected-result">Expected result</h2>

The selected campaign supplies its notes, sessions, compiled rules, and subscribed books as possible context for a question. A collection can be shared by several campaigns, but its subscription switch is set separately for each one.

<h2 id="example">Example</h2>

Choose **Lanterns of Greyharbor**, subscribe it to **Greyharbor Gazetteer**, and record the ferrymaster **Mara Venn** in its notebook. Then ask:

> What does Mara know about the bell beneath North Quay?

Chronacle can use Mara's saved campaign notes together with relevant passages from the subscribed gazetteer and show a source badge such as `Greyharbor Gazetteer.pdf p.42`.

<h2 id="limits">Limits</h2>

- A campaign does not own every PDF it can search; access comes from collection subscriptions.
- Switching campaigns reloads that campaign's chat history.
- Learn how to create, rename, or delete one in [Manage campaigns](/en/manual/campaigns/manage).
