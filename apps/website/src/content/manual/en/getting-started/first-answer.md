<h2 id="ask-and-check">Ask and check</h2>

Ask one specific question in the Oracle, then use its source badge to check the answer against your PDF.

1. Confirm that your campaign is selected and subscribed to the collection containing the source.
2. Open **Oracle** and click the field labelled **Ask a rule, a name, a place…**
3. Type a focused question and press Enter, or select **Send**.
4. Read the reply, then select a source badge such as **Moonlit Roads.pdf p.31**.
5. Compare the answer with the **Source passage** popover.

<h2 id="expected-result">Expected result</h2>

The answer appears in the conversation with one or more source badges; selecting a badge shows its source name, page, and supporting text when available.

<h2 id="example">Example</h2>

With `Moonlit Roads.pdf` indexed in the subscribed **Roads and Ruins** collection, type exactly:

> What breaks the oath that binds Captain Ilyra to the moon bridge?

An answer might explain that speaking Ilyra's childhood name at dawn breaks the oath, with:

> [Source: "Moonlit Roads.pdf", p.31]

Open the badge and make sure the passage supports both the name and the timing before using the ruling at the table.

<h2 id="if-the-answer-is-weak">If the answer is weak</h2>

- Use names and terms that appear in the source. A shorter, more specific question often retrieves a better passage.
- Check that the book shows **Indexed** and that the active campaign subscribes to its collection.
- A citation shows where the answer came from; it is still worth reading the passage when wording matters.
- For cross-language questions, review [language and search settings](/en/manual/ai-providers/language-and-search).
