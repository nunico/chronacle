# Source Collections

## What collections are

A collection is a named shelf for your PDFs. You create it once, give it a name like "D&D 5e Core Rules" or "GM Advice", and upload whichever rulebooks belong there. Collections live independently of any campaign — you can delete a campaign without touching its collections, and the same collection can serve a dozen different campaigns at once.

## Setting up your first collection

Open the **Library** tab. In the left sidebar under "Collections", click the **+** button and type a name — for example, "D&D 5e Core Rules". Press Enter or click Create.

Now upload a PDF into it. Click **Upload PDF** in the top bar. Your file picker opens. After you choose a file, a small dialog asks which collection to add it to. Select "D&D 5e Core Rules" from the dropdown and click **Upload**. Chronacle indexes the PDF in the background; a progress bar shows the work moving along. When it finishes, the source appears listed under that collection.

To wire a campaign to the collection, click the campaign name in the left sidebar under "Campaigns". The main area shows "Source Collections" with a row of chips. Click **+ D&D 5e Core Rules** to subscribe. That campaign can now search everything you've put in the collection.

## Sharing sources across campaigns

Once a PDF is indexed in a collection, every campaign subscribed to that collection can search it — no re-uploading needed.

For example: you have "Waterdeep Dragon Heist" and "Curse of Strahd" both subscribed to "D&D 5e Core Rules". If Strahd's player asks what a spell does, Chronacle searches the same Player's Handbook that Dragon Heist uses. The PDF was processed once and the two campaigns simply share it.

## Keeping settings separate

Different settings — even within the same game system — can produce very different lore. You probably don't want Forgotten Realms geography answers leaking into your Dark Sun campaign.

The fix is simple: create a collection for each setting. Put the Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide in "D&D 5e Forgotten Realms" and your Dark Sun sourcebook in "D&D 5e Dark Sun". Subscribe "Waterdeep Dragon Heist" only to "D&D 5e Core Rules" and "D&D 5e Forgotten Realms". Subscribe "Dark Sun: Last King" only to "D&D 5e Core Rules" and "D&D 5e Dark Sun". Neither campaign sees the other's setting material.

## Uploading campaign-specific material

Some PDFs — a custom adventure, a set of handouts, your session prep notes exported as a PDF — belong to one campaign only. Create a dedicated collection for it: "Waterdeep Dragon Heist — Prep", upload your materials there, and subscribe only the Dragon Heist campaign to it. No other campaign will ever search that collection.

## What gets searched when you ask a question

When you type a question in the chat, Chronacle searches only the collections your active campaign subscribes to. Nothing from unsubscribed collections ever appears in an answer — not even if two campaigns share the same app. If your Dragon Heist campaign is active, it cannot reach sources that belong exclusively to your Dark Sun game, and vice versa.

## Renaming a collection

In the sidebar, hover over the collection name. A small pencil icon appears to the right. Click it, type the new name, and press Enter. The name updates everywhere it appears.

## Deleting a collection

Hover over the collection in the sidebar and click the **X** icon. Chronacle will block the deletion — and show you why — if any campaign is still subscribed to the collection or if it still contains sources. Remove the sources first (click the X on each one in the collection's source list), then unsubscribe any campaigns (open each campaign and remove the chip), and then delete the collection.
